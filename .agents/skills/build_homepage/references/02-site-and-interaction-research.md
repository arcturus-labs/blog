# 02 — Research positioning and interactions separately

## Two different research questions

1. **Comparable businesses:** how do they explain their value, organize paths, package offers, establish credibility, and convert visitors? See `ai_agency_review/README.md`, comparison, navigation, and subject notes.
2. **Engaging websites:** what specific behavior helps someone understand, explore, or dwell? See `engaging_websites/README.md`, `patterns.md`, and `method.md`.

A technically impressive engineering explainer is not automatically useful inspiration for a consulting site. Don't substitute complexity for relevance. Avoid generic glowing AI decoration, dated logo strips, and literal rectangular cards merely because they are common.

## Inspect behavior, not just screenshots

- Observe the live page and identify the exact section the owner likes.
- Scroll through its entire activation/release sequence. Hover/click with real browser actions.
- Inspect layout, sticky bounds, transitions, transforms, and timing as needed.
- Distinguish verified behavior from an inferred implementation. A screenshot establishes a state, not how it got there.
- Note accessible/keyboard equivalents and what happens on touch; don't claim mobile validation from desktop inspection.

Keep findings small: a case note with URL, pattern, measurements when useful, screenshot references, relevance, and limits. Synthesize the reusable principle; do not reproduce the reference's whole layout or copy.

## What mattered in this project

Ponder provided two especially helpful patterns:
- Expanding choices with a clear hover-revealed invitation.
- A stable outline highlighting the active step while corresponding scenes change with scroll.

The owner adapted these into a homepage chooser and separate problem/solution stories. Later feedback refined the story further: separate problem and solution sequences, fixed left headings, and right-side explanation/imagery so text expansion doesn't make the outline jump.

Stars/brand imagery can carry an emotional arc, but the visitor should understand the story through ordinary scrolling. Hover flourishes are optional. Fine motion polish comes after content and composition.

Extract the behavior, not an immutable visual prescription. Here the owner later preferred a continuous star field with perceptible depth-based scroll parallax over a nearly starless problem section. A larger "Arcturus Labs can help" title did not mean it should be separated from the books/quotes. A "slide pause" means useful reading dwell, not permission to change a liked composition. Compare before/after screenshots at the same viewport when adapting a reference.
