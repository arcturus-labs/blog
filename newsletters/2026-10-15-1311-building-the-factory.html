Two Takes on Software Factories

The Dark Factory: How Twin Sun Automated Their Entire Dev Pipeline

Twin Sun's automated reviewer approves about 70% of pull requests without a human. In my conversation with their CEO and CTO, two principles stood out: constrain the factory to how your team actually builds software, and earn trust one component at a time before letting it run on its own.
