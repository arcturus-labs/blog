# Allie K. Miller: review notes

Reviewed 2026-10-29, desktop 1280px. Pages: home (full), contact route followed by link only. Files: `text.md` (raw page text; optional), `screenshots/02-home-above-fold.png`.
Platform: Squarespace. Business type: celebrity advisor/speaker plus courses and email list.

## Above the fold
- Full-bleed photo of Allie mid-talk, name in huge white type, one-line descriptor ("Artificial Intelligence Business Leader and International Speaker in New York City, NY"), one yellow button: CONTACT ALLIE. Cookie banner covers the bottom.
- Black announcement bar above the nav: "Join 60,000+ in my FREE 5-day AI Fast Track email course."
- The identity is a person and a role (speaker). It doesn't say what problem she solves. Credibility is conveyed by the photo, name, and later by logos.

## Navigation and structure
- Standard nav: Home, Services, Courses, Resources, Contact, Login, social icons, cart. The home page is a long scroll with sections: About, Experience, free course, client logos, Products, Public Speaking, Reviews, Advising CTA, Gallery, Subscribe.
- Contact is a one-click nav item and also the hero button.

## How the offer is packaged
- Four lines of business: speaking (200+ talks, booked via an agency page), advising ("a speaking or advising session"), courses (AI-First Academy; third-party LinkedIn Learning and MasterClass), free 5-day email course.
- Advising is a short paragraph with no price, no format, and no process. Courses are clear products.

## Proof
- The strongest proof stack of the sites so far: Time 100 AI, named clients (Novartis, Samsung, Salesforce, Coca-Cola, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic), a 23-logo carousel, 200+ talks, audience lists, followers (~2M), press logos, and a long awards list.
- Reviews are attributed by company or role (Thomson Reuters, Salesforce, SAS, "Director, $60B company"). Roles/companies make them credible; they are speaker reviews, not advisory-client outcomes.
- Experience section is credential-heavy prose (a wall of awards).

## Conversion
- Contact button in hero, nav, and mid-page ("REQUEST NOW" / "BOOK ALLIE"); free 5-day email course promoted in a top banner, mid-page, and footer. Product SIGN UP buttons.
- Primary email capture = free email course (60,000+ claimed). Contact goes to a separate request page.
- Email address is visible in the footer (support@alliekmiller.com).

## Voice and tone
- Third person biography ("Allie Miller is a top AI leader"), polished, superlative-heavy. Marketing-corporate, but the footer tagline is plain: "Turning AI buzzwords into business tactics."

## Agent-readiness
- robots.txt is Squarespace's default, listing many AI crawlers (AI2Bot, anthropic-ai, ClaudeBot, CCBot, etc.). I didn't read the full rules.
- sitemap.xml exists (39 URLs, last modified 2026-10-23). No llms.txt, no `.well-known` agent files, no feed. No JSON-LD checked.

## Freshness
- Sitemap updated 2026-10-23, so the site is maintained. Content leans on older awards (2019-2023) and speaking history.

## Takeaways relevant to Arcturus
- Credibility stack and a visible Contact button work, but the "what do you help with" is thin: it is a bio-led site.
- A free email course as the lead magnet is the dominant conversion tool (60,000+ claimed).
- Clear separation of products from speaking/advising is helpful.
- Weak spots to avoid: wall-of-awards prose, advising with no description or first step.
