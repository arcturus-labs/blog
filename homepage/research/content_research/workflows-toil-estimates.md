# Corporate workflow toil: time and economic opportunity

Research 2026-11-02. Discussion only; no approved website copy. Distinguish perceived low-value time, technical automation potential, modeled economic value and actually realized savings. None is automatically equivalent to wasted payroll recoverable with agents.

## Best fit: Slack Workforce Lab (January 2024)

Full primary article and methodology retrieved:
https://slack.com/blog/news/new-slack-research-shows-accelerating-ai-use-at-work

10,281 full-time desk workers across US, Australia, France, Germany, Japan and UK; fielded January 10–29, 2024 via Qualtrics. Not targeted to Slack/Salesforce employees or customers.

Exact statement:
> Desk workers report spending 41% of their time on tasks that are “low value, repetitive or lack meaningful contribution to their core job functions.”

Self-reported vendor-sponsored survey, not time tracking or task-by-task automation feasibility assessment. 41% of a 40-hour week is 16.4 hours (our arithmetic), not demonstrated recoverable savings. Do not say 41% is automatable or wasted.

Relevant exact quote, Christina Janzer, Slack SVP of research and analytics / head of Workforce Lab:
> “We all have tasks to complete that aren’t part of our job description but are necessary to keep things running smoothly. It’s the ‘work of work,’”

Suggested attributed website wording: “In Slack’s survey of more than 10,000 desk workers, respondents said 41% of their time went to low-value, repetitive tasks or work that didn’t meaningfully contribute to their core role.” Candidate only.

## Asana Anatomy of Work (2023)

Primary links reviewed through search excerpts; full press release blocked (403):
https://investors.asana.com/news-releases/news-release-details/asana-anatomy-work-global-index-2023-smart-collaboration-and
https://asana.com/resources/anatomy-of-work

9,615 global knowledge workers. Survey reports 58% of time on work coordination / “work about work” rather than skilled strategic tasks. Respondents estimate better processes could save 4.9 hours weekly. Search-excerpt-level verification only; re-fetch release and methods before publication.

Coordination includes necessary work; neither all waste nor all automatable. Perceived 4.9-hour opportunity is NOT observed AI savings. Would use Slack in preference: fully retrieved, clearer language and direct fit.

## McKinsey technical automation potential (June 2023)

Primary report/search extracts:
https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier
Alternative legacy URL: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier

Report says current generative AI AND OTHER technologies have the potential to automate activities absorbing 60–70% of employee time. Analysis covers component activities, not elimination of entire jobs. Technical capability model, not measured deployment results, waste estimate or software-agent-only claim; includes non-office work and other automation technology. Page and primary PDF timed out in fetch; search excerpts reviewed. Use as broad context, not narrow expert-workflow claim.

Across 63 generative-AI use cases, estimates $2.6–4.4 trillion in potential annual value globally. This is modeled economic value across industries (not all cost savings; not current losses or cash waiting to be recovered). Primary search excerpts also available at:
https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/capturing-the-full-value-of-generative-ai-in-banking

## Realized estimate: Salesforce internal deployment

Full primary self-report retrieved:
https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/how-salesforce-uses-ai/

Salesforce says its Einstein Slack app processed nearly 370,000 queries in a quarter and saved users an estimated 50,000 hours combined. Tasks include meeting scheduling/summarization, general questions and offsite planning. No estimation method/sample controls provided in article. Vendor describing its own technology internally, not independently verified savings or proof all requests are agentic workflows. Nonetheless more tangible corporate example than a global economic forecast.

## Recommendation

For the pain section use Slack's 41% estimate with visible attribution and self-report framing. If a concrete dollar illustration is wanted, compute a clearly labeled hypothetical from a specified team, loaded hourly cost and genuinely recovered hours; never multiply all low-value time by salary and call it guaranteed ROI. For evidence that a structured expert-reviewed workflow can help, pair the pain with JSM's legal first-draft results in workflows-success-cases.md rather than implying Slack measured agentic benefits.
