## Solution: the virtuous cycle

### 1. Find the right starting point.

Look for valuable, repeatable tasks with outcomes your experts can check. Watch how employees already use AI, then capture the most promising processes as agent skills.

*Visual: fruit tree with low-hanging fruit.*

### 2. Augment your experts.

Let agents gather information, research options and prepare first drafts. Give experts the sources, findings and open questions they need to review the work rather than start from scratch.

**15% more issues resolved per hour**, on average, in a study of 5,172 AI-assisted customer-support workers.

[Generative AI at Work · Quarterly Journal of Economics, 2025](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/generative-ai-work)

*Visual: cyborg on the left handing a stack of paperwork to a human professional on the right.*

*Additional evidence moment on the same outline step and stationary image:*

**40–60% less time preparing first-pass advice.**

Johnson Stokes & Master reports that result for standard employment queries, while lawyers still review and finalize the advice.

> “The output provides a genuinely strong starting point, allowing our lawyers to spend more time on the strategic judgement and client counsel that matter most.”

[Joe Choy, Partner, Johnson Stokes & Master · Microsoft customer story](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/features/from-legacy-to-ai-frontier-how-johnson-stokes-master-built-a-governance-first-copilot-model-for-regulated-legal-services/)

### 3. Promote what proves reliable.

Capture expert corrections and identify which kinds of tasks consistently meet the standard. Automate the reliable, low-risk cases; keep the rest under review. Your organization decides where to draw the line.

*Visual: relaxed, confident human expert on the left reviewing a document; cyborg on the right independently handling business work.*

### 4. Improve without starting over.

Turn reviewed examples into evaluation tests. When models, tools or instructions change, rerun familiar cases to see what improved and what regressed – before expanding automation.

Your expertise and test cases carry forward, even as the technology changes.

*Visual: cyborgs ascending stairs; the lowest agent hands paperwork up to the next agent.*
