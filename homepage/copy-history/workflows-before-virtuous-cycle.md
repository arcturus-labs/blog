## The solution

### Capture how your experts work, in plain English

Interview your expert and write their steps, exceptions, and escalation rules as a [plain-English skill](https://agentskills.io/home). {gold:Something they can read, correct, and own.}

*Image alt:* A plain-English process document titled Find a substitute product lists numbered steps, a rule to stop and ask a person for controlled substances, and a comment showing the clinical analyst edited step three.

### Start with expert review

The AI drafts, with its sources, steps, and open questions on the page. Experts approve, repair, or reject. {gold:Their time shifts from producing the work to reviewing it.}

Practitioners start by reviewing 20–50 real outputs. [Husain and Shankar, AI Evals FAQ](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/)

*Image alt:* A review screen shows an AI draft with linked sources, steps taken, and an open question, beside Approve, Repair, and Reject buttons and an expert comment. A red handwritten note reads: Experts review. They don’t start from scratch.

### Earn more autonomy, one task at a time

Expert corrections become test cases, and every change to a skill or model is rerun against them. Tasks that prove reliable can run on their own. {gold:The rest stay with your experts.}

*Image alt:* An autonomy board sorts tasks into Runs automatically, Needs expert review, and Not yet. A side strip shows expert corrections feeding a set of test cases.
