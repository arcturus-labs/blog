The solution

  1. 01Make your platform discoverable and addressable by agents
  2. 02Be present for your customers when the conversation begins
  3. 03Build an engaging experience that brings the user and their AI assistant back to your platform

Make your platform discoverable and addressable by agents

Make your platform easy for agents to find and understand – with accessible content, clear documentation, and discoverable integrations and skills.

Be present for your customers when the conversation begins

When users start their journey in chat, meet them there with useful tools, skills, and integrations that help them accomplish their goals. When possible, carry the conversation into your website – into an experience you own.

Build an engaging experience that brings the user and their AI assistant back to your platform

In the future, everyone will have a personal AI assistant that carries their preferences, context, and past conversations. Don't make them leave it at the door.

On your platform, the assistant can usher the customer through the interface, bring in insights from background research, and adapt the experience to suit the customer's needs.

The assistant observes the customer interact with the page, works alongside them, and brings relevant information into view.
